# Effects and layers

Effects are grid-aware visual markers for areas, lines, cones, measurements, and status markers. They become available when a grid is visible and locked to physical one-inch cells, ensuring their geometry corresponds to the tabletop.

## Place an effect

1. Lock the grid and open **Effects & layers**.
2. Set the color, opacity, optional label, visibility, and whether to show its distance.
3. Choose whether **Keep as layer** is enabled.
4. Open the effects palette with the shortcut or by secondary-clicking the map; touch users can long-press.
5. Choose a shape and drag on the map to place it. Shapes snap to appropriate grid centers.

The palette supports common tactical geometry, including lines, squares, circles, cones, and measurements. Cone placement begins by setting its axis, then uses the next point to set width. Measurements are DM-only rulers; they are not synchronized as player-facing layers.

## Temporary versus persistent

With **Keep as layer** on, the completed effect is saved to the selected scene. With it off, a public effect is a temporary flash: it is useful for calling attention to an area without leaving map clutter. Private effects never appear on the player display.

## Manage layers

The layer list shows each permanent effect by its label or shape type. Select one to make it active, toggle its table visibility, focus the map on it, or delete it. **Select on map** lets you choose an existing effect directly from the stage; adjust the draft controls and choose **Update layer** to apply the new style.

Persistent effects are saved with their scene. Use them for spell zones, hazards, cover, or room annotations that should reappear whenever the scene is published.
